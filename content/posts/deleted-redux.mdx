---
title: I finally deleted our Redux store
date: 2026-07-20
tag: front-end
excerpt: Three years of reducers, selectors and action creators for state that mostly just... mirrors the server.
readMins: 4
---

Most of what our Redux store held was server data with a local cache slapped on top — the exact job a data-fetching library already does better. Stripping it out wasn't a rewrite, it was a subtraction: swap the async thunks for query hooks, keep the two or three genuinely client-only slices (a wizard's current step, a draft form), and delete the rest.

The store went from 40-odd reducers to three. Bundle size dropped, sure, but the bigger win was onboarding — new hires used to spend their first week just tracing an action through the reducer tree. Now they read a component and see the data it needs.

Redux wasn't wrong for 2019. It's just rarely the right default in 2026, when the server-state problem has better tools built for exactly that problem.
